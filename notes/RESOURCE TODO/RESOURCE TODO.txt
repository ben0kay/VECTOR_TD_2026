# VECTOR TD 2026 — RESOURCE & MANUFACTURING PLAN

## Core direction

VECTOR TD uses a semi-realistic industrial economy presented with fast, readable arcade rules.

The fortress begins with simple credit-based construction. As a level progresses, mining, refining and fabrication unlock more powerful structures, ammunition and technology.

The economy has four layers:

1. Raw resources are mined.
2. Refineries process raw resources.
3. Fabricators manufacture components and consumables.
4. Towers and buildings consume those materials.

The system should provide simulation depth without requiring excessive micromanagement.

---

## Resource tiers

### Universal currency

#### Credits

The universal construction currency.

Used by:

- Every building and tower
- Repairs
- Upgrades
- Research
- Services and special actions

Early structures can cost only Credits. More advanced structures require Credits plus resources or components.

---

## Foundational raw resources

### Carbon

Primary structural resource.

Used for:

- Walls
- Foundations
- Storage buildings
- Armour
- Structural frames
- Plating

Processed into:

- Carbon Composite

---

### Copper

Primary conductive resource.

Used for:

- Power cables
- Energy nodes
- Motors
- Weapon coils
- Ammunition
- Basic electronics

Processed into:

- Conductive Alloy

---

### Silicon

Primary computational resource.

Used for:

- Targeting systems
- Radar
- Automation
- Research
- Advanced tower control
- Processing equipment

Processed into:

- Silicon Wafers

---

### Lithium

Primary energy-storage resource.

Used for:

- Batteries
- Building energy buffers
- Shield systems
- Drones
- High-energy weapons
- Emergency power reserves

Processed into:

- Lithium Cells

---

### Ferrite Ore

Iron-rich magnetic ore used for machinery and electromagnetic systems.

Used for:

- Turret mechanisms
- Motors
- Generators
- Transformers
- Heavy ammunition
- Magnetic weapons

Processed into:

- Ferrite Cores

Ferrite is based on real magnetic iron materials but is simplified for the arcade setting.

---

### Vectorite

A rare crystalline material formed where physical infrastructure, energy and hostile digital corruption intersect.

Vectorite is the primary late-game ore.

Used for:

- Ion weapons
- Advanced shields
- Anti-phasing technology
- Quantum processing
- Teleportation
- Extreme energy storage
- Endgame buildings
- CPU upgrades
- Campaign research

Processed into:

- Stabilized Vectorite

Vectorite should be rare, visually distinctive and introduced in later levels.

## Refined materials

The refinery converts raw resources into standardized industrial materials.

Suggested baseline recipes:

| Recipe | Input | Output | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Carbon Composite | 100 Carbon | 1 | 25 sec |
| Conductive Alloy | 100 Copper | 1 | 30 sec |
| Silicon Wafer | 100 Silicon | 1 | 35 sec |
| Lithium Cell | 100 Lithium | 1 | 40 sec |
| Ferrite Core | 100 Ferrite Ore | 1 | 35 sec |
| Stabilized Vectorite | 100 Vectorite | 1 | 90 sec |

These conversion values are starting balance values and can be changed per recipe later.

Vectorite processing should:

- Take considerably longer
- Consume substantial energy
- Possibly require an advanced refinery
- Produce a major alert or notification when completed

## Fabricated components

The fabricator combines refined materials into construction components.

### Light Plating

Ingredients:

- 1 Carbon Composite
- 1 Conductive Alloy

Used for:

- Standard towers
- Storage buildings
- Drones
- Light defensive structures
- Ammunition production

---

### Heavy Plating

Ingredients:

- 2 Carbon Composite
- 1 Ferrite Core

Used for:

- Heavy towers
- Reinforced walls
- Artillery
- Large generators
- Shield generators
- High-end buildings

---

### Ammunition Casing

Ingredients:

- 1 Conductive Alloy
- 1 Ferrite Core

Output:

- Multiple casings per batch

Used for:

- Bullets
- Cannon shells
- Rockets
- Other physical ammunition

Casings should be shared between ammunition recipes and some weapon construction recipes.

---

### Precision Components

Ingredients:

### Ion Charge

Ingredients:

- Stabilized Vectorite
- Energy Assembly
- Reactor Casing

Output:

- A small number of Ion Charges

Used by:

- Ion cannons
- Major anti-siege weapons
- Endgame defensive structures

Ion Charges should be expensive but extremely powerful.

## Building cost rules

### Early game

Use Credits only or Credits plus a small amount of raw material.

Examples:

- Basic tower
- Basic wall
- Standard foundation
- Basic miner
- Basic storage

### Mid game

Use Credits plus raw resources or one refined material.

Examples:

- Minigun
- Cannon
- Radar
- Battery
- Repair station
- Shield generator
- Refinery

### Late game

Use Credits plus several fabricated components.

Examples:

- Artillery
- Advanced shield generator
- Large battery
- Advanced refinery
- Fabricator upgrades
- Ion cannon

### Endgame

Use Credits, fabricated components and Vectorite technology.

Examples:

- Ion cannon
- Quantum targeting array
- Anti-phasing field
- Teleportation relay
- Fortress-wide shield
- CPU superstructure upgrades

Ordinary buildings should avoid requiring more than three different ingredients.

## Storage approach

Each raw resource retains its own dedicated storage building.

For now, a resource’s storage can hold both its raw and refined forms separately:

```text
Silicon Storage
- Raw Silicon
- Silicon Wafers
```

Shared industrial storage can hold fabricated components:

```text
Component Storage
- Plating
- Casings
- Precision Components
- Energy Assemblies
- Reactor Casings
- Vector Cores
```

Ammunition Storage holds:

- Bullets
- Explosives
- Cannon shells
- Ion Charges

This avoids requiring a separate building for every individual component.

## Gameplay progression

### Early level

- Mine common resources
- Construct basic defenses using Credits
- Establish storage and energy
- Survive baseline enemies

### Developing fortress

- Build refineries
- Introduce material costs
- Produce ammunition
- Establish batteries and local energy networks
- Build specialized towers

### Advanced fortress

- Construct fabricators
- Manufacture plating, casings and precision components
- Support shields, artillery and advanced industry
- Manage drones and material delivery

### Late game

- Locate and mine Vectorite
- Protect Vectorite operations
- Build advanced processing infrastructure
- Manufacture Vector Cores and Ion Charges
- Deploy endgame defenses

---

## Design guardrails

- Credits remain useful throughout the entire game.
- Basic defenses must never require a fully developed factory chain.
- Raw resources support common construction.
- Refined materials support specialized buildings.
- Fabricated components support advanced technology.
- Vectorite supports rare and transformative technology.
- Avoid creating components without multiple meaningful uses.
- Avoid giving ordinary buildings overly complicated recipes.
- Production chains should create strategic decisions, not repetitive busywork.
- Every resource should have a recognizable color, icon and gameplay identity.
- Exact costs remain data-driven and adjustable per building, recipe and level.
